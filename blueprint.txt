# Portfolio Website Blueprint

This document outlines the structure and content for a personal portfolio website.

## 1. Home Page (Landing)
**Goal:** Create a strong first impression and introduce who you are.
- **Hero Section:**
  - Large, bold typography with your name and title (e.g., "Creative Developer", "UI/UX Designer").
        Aditya Kumar Maurya, Full Stack Developer, App Developer, 
  - Short, punchy tagline or value proposition.
  - Call to Action (CTA) buttons: "View Work" and "Contact Me".
  - Dynamic background or hero image/3D element.
- **Brief Intro:** A 2-3 sentence summary of your expertise.
- **Featured Work Preview:** A carousel or grid showing 2-3 top projects.

## 2. About Me Page
**Goal:** Share your story, personality, and professional journey.
- **Bio:** detailed introduction, your background, education, and what drives you.
- **Photo:** Professional yet personable headshot or creative portrait.
- **Experience/Timeline:** Visual timeline of your career highlights and roles.
- **Personal Interests:** Brief section on hobbies or interests to humanize you.
- **Resume Download:** Button to download PDF resume.

## 3. Projects / Portfolio Page
**Goal:** Showcase your best work with detailed case studies.
- **Project Grid:** Filterable grid (e.g., by category: Web, Mobile, Design) of project thumbnails.
- **Project Cards:** Each card includes:
  - Project Title
  - Short description
  - Tech stack icons
  - "View Project" link
- **Case Study Detail (Template for each project):**
  - **Overview:** Problem statement and solution.
  - **Role:** What you did.
  - **Tech Stack:** Tools and languages used.
  - **Screenshots/Demo:** High-quality images or video walkthroughs.
  - **Links:** GitHub repo and Live Demo.

## 4. Skills & Expertise Page
**Goal:** Highlight technical capabilities and tools.
- **Technical Skills:** Categorized lists (e.g., Frontend, Backend, Tools) with proficiency bars or icons.
- **Soft Skills:** Communication, Leadership, Problem Solving.
- **Certifications:** Badges or list of completed courses/certs.
- **Services (Optional):** If freelancing, list what you offer (e.g., "Website Design", "Consulting").

## 5. Contact Page
**Goal:** Make it easy for people to reach you.
- **Contact Form:** Name, Email, Subject, Message fields.
- **Direct Info:** Email address, Location (City/Country).
- **Social Links:** LinkedIn, GitHub, Twitter/X, Behance, etc.
- **Availability Status:** "Currently open to new opportunities" or "Booked until [Month]".

## 6. Blog / Insights (Optional)
**Goal:** Demonstrate thought leadership and share knowledge.
- **Article List:** Recent posts with titles, dates, and excerpts.
- **Categories/Tags:** To organize content.

## Global Elements (Header/Footer)
- **Navigation Bar:** Logo, Links to pages, Theme Toggle (Light/Dark mode).
- **Footer:** Copyright, Social Links, Quick links.
